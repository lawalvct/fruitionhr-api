## Phase 0 — Discovery & System Design ✅

**Goal:** agree exactly what will be built before building it.

- Requirements reviewed against your feature list
- Full module plan and system architecture prepared
- Brand identity applied (logo, colours, look & feel)
- Phased delivery roadmap (this document)

## Marketing Website ✅

**Goal:** a professional public face for FruitionHR from day one.

- Home page with product presentation
- Features page covering every module
- Pricing page
- Mobile-friendly, fast, and ready for your domain

## Phase 1 — Platform Foundation & Security 🔄

**Goal:** the secure base every other feature stands on.

- Company sign-up: each company gets its own private, isolated workspace
- Secure staff login with protection against password attacks
- Roles & permissions (e.g. Owner, HR Admin, Manager, Employee) — salary information has its own separate permission
- Company dashboard and the platform administration area
- Audit trail: every sensitive action is recorded

*Already working: company registration, login, roles & permissions, dashboard shells for both the company and platform-admin areas.*

## Phase 2 — Company Setup & Employee Records

**Goal:** your organisation structure and a complete employee database.

- Branches, departments, positions, job grades and employment types
- Employee profiles: biodata, contacts, next of kin, education, bank, tax & pension details
- Employee documents & contracts with expiry reminders
- Employment history — promotions, transfers and grade changes tracked over time
- Bulk import of existing employees from Excel

## Phase 3 — Approvals, Documents & Notifications

**Goal:** requests flow to the right people automatically.

- Configurable multi-step approvals (e.g. Employee → Supervisor → HR)
- Central, secure document storage across the system
- In-app and email notifications for requests, approvals and reminders
- Complete approval history on every request

## Phase 4 — Attendance & Leave

**Goal:** know who is present, late or on leave — automatically.

- Shift setup and rosters
- Clock in/out with manual entry and bulk import
- Automatic flagging of lateness, early exit and absence
- Leave types, policies and automatic balance tracking
- Leave requests with approvals and a team leave calendar

## Phase 5 — Payroll & Statutory Compliance 🏁 Milestone 1

**Goal:** accurate, compliant salaries at the click of a button. **At the end of this phase the system is in live use.**

- Flexible salary structures — basic, housing, transport and custom components
- PAYE, Pension, NHF and NSITF calculated automatically
- Loans and salary advances with automatic repayment deductions
- Payroll preview → approval → lock (no silent changes, ever)
- Payslips (PDF), bank payment schedules and statutory remittance reports

## Phase 6 — Advanced Payroll

**Goal:** handle every payroll situation, not just the routine month.

- Payroll corrections through proper reversals and adjustments
- 13th month pay and bonuses
- Gratuity and final settlement for exiting staff
- Payroll variance report (this month vs last month, explained)
- Payroll journal export for your accounting software

## Phase 7 — Employee & Manager Self-Service 🏁 Milestone 2

**Goal:** staff serve themselves; HR stops being a help desk.

- Employees: view payslips, apply for leave, check attendance, request loans, update their profile
- Managers: team dashboard, approvals inbox, team attendance and leave calendar
- Works on phones — no app installation needed

## Phase 8 — Recruitment & Onboarding 🏁 Milestone 3

**Goal:** from vacancy to first day in one organised pipeline.

- Staff requisitions with approval before a vacancy opens
- Job postings and applicant tracking through interview stages
- Interview scheduling, panels and score sheets
- Offer letters and acceptance tracking
- Onboarding checklist; the hired candidate becomes an employee in one click

## Phase 9 — Performance & Goals 🏁 Milestone 4

**Goal:** structured, fair performance management.

- Appraisal cycles with self, manager and 360° reviews
- KPI library with weights and custom rating scales
- Company, department and individual goals with check-ins
- Automatic scoring, grading and promotion/training recommendations

## Phase 10 — Training & Development

**Goal:** plan and track employee growth.

- Training needs, calendar and budgets
- Session attendance and assessments
- Certificates and training cost tracking

## Phase 11 — Enterprise HR

**Goal:** complete the employee lifecycle.

- Disciplinary case management (query → hearing → decision → appeal)
- Exit management: resignation, clearance, final pay, exit interview
- Company assets: assignment, return and clearance
- Succession planning for critical positions

## Phase 12 — Advanced Reports & AI Assistance

**Goal:** decision support on top of your data.

- Analytics dashboards: headcount, payroll cost trends, attendance and attrition
- Custom report builder
- AI assistance: job description drafts, appraisal summaries, payroll variance explanations and more

## Notes

- **Timelines:** dates for each phase will be agreed together after the scope of Phases 1–5 is confirmed. Phases can overlap where practical.
- **You'll see progress continuously** — each phase ends with a working demonstration on the live staging environment, not just a report.
- **Change requests** are welcome and will be scheduled into the phase plan so they never destabilise payroll accuracy.
- **Future roadmap** (after Phase 12): mobile app for employees, biometric device integration, and multi-country payroll.
